THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE RARE BOOKS
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge (auction records, ABAA dealer lore, census of
known copies), not live price data. Five binder pages of up to 50: Antiquarian (before 1800, plus
the great colour-plate books), 19th-Century Classics, Modern Firsts (1920+ literary fiction),
Genre Firsts (fantasy, science fiction, crime and horror), and Children's & Illustrated.
First release ships 20 per page (100); each page grows to 50 later without code changes (append
lines, rerun tools/gen_worlds.py books). Order is roughly most to least desirable as a first edition.
Signatures, inscriptions, book-club copies and ex-library copies are left to the variant roll in game.
Format: rank. Title | Why it matters | Year | Page | kind (book / booklot) | tags | Author | Publisher | Wikipedia titles (; = fallbacks)
Tags: debut = the author's first book, banned = banned or seized somewhere, jacket = the dust jacket is half the value.
