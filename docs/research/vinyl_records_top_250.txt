THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE VINYL RECORDS
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge (auction records, record-fair dealer lore,
Goldmine price-guide lore), not live price data. Five binder pages of up to 50, by crate: Rock &
Pop, Jazz & Blues, Soul & Funk, Punk, Indie & Metal, and Hip-Hop & Dance.
First release ships 20 per page (100); each page grows to 50 later without code changes (append
lines, rerun tools/gen_worlds.py vinyl). Order is roughly most to least desirable as an original
pressing. Promos, test pressings, mono mixes, coloured vinyl and reissues are left to the variant
roll in game.
Format: rank. Title | Why it matters | Year | Page | kind (record / crate) | tags | Artist | Label | Wikipedia titles (; = fallbacks)
Tags: debut = the artist's first release, single = a single (45, 78 or 12-inch), twelve = a
12-inch single, 78 = a ten-inch shellac 78, banned = pulled or withdrawn. No single tag = an LP.
