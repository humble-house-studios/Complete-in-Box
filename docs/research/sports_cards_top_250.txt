THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE SPORTS CARDS
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge (PSA population and sold-price lore), not
live price data. Five binder pages of up to 50: baseball, basketball, football, hockey, and
soccer & other sports. First release ships 20 per page (100); each page grows to 50 later
without code changes (append lines, rerun tools/gen_worlds.py sports).
Order within each page is roughly most to least desirable. Parallels, autos and refractors are
left to the variant roll in game, except where the card IS the parallel (Superfractor, Ticket).
Format: rank. Player | Set / card | Year | Page | kind (card / wax) | tags | Team | Position | Wikipedia title
Tags: rc = rookie card, error = known error card, ss = short print, auto = signed in the set.
