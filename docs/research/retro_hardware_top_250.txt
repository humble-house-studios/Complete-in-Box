THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE RETRO CONSOLES AND ACCESSORIES
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge, not live price data. Weighted toward Nintendo, Sony, Microsoft and Dreamcast hardware, with a smaller sampling of Sega, Atari and other systems. Includes consoles, limited-edition colors, controllers, peripherals and prototypes. Order within each section is roughly most to least desirable (approximate, not a strict price ranking). Check current sold listings (eBay, PriceCharting, Heritage) before buying or pricing.
