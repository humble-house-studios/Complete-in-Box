THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE PIECES OF POP CULTURE
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge (Heritage, Hake's, Profiles in History, Bonhams,
Christie's and Sotheby's auction records, toy-show dealer lore and the price guides), not live price
data. Five binder pages of up to 50: Action Figures, Toys & Fads, Props & Costumes, Posters, and
Lunchboxes & Keepsakes.
First release ships 20 per page (100); each page grows to 50 later without code changes (append
lines, rerun tools/gen_worlds.py pop). Order is roughly most to least valuable in top condition.
Re-releases, store exclusives, reproduction cards and prototypes are left to the variant roll in game.
Format: rank. Name | Why it matters (the real-world record) | Year | Page | kind (toy / prop / poster / toybox) | tags | Maker or studio | Form | Wikipedia titles (; = fallbacks)
Tags: screen = used on screen (props and costumes), fad = a craze toy, premium = a mail-away or
giveaway, proto = a prototype. Forms pick the 3D model and the packaging: carded (blister card),
boxed (window box), figure / doll / plush / vehicle (loose models), prop, costume, hat, poster,
lunchbox, dispenser, badge, lamp, chest (a box lot).
