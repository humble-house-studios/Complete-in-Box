THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE RARE COINS
Compiled Nov 2026 from general numismatic knowledge, not live price data. US copper, nickel,
silver and gold, plus world and ancient coins. Order within each section is roughly most to least
desirable (approximate, not a strict price ranking); the lower part of each section is the sort of
coin a beginner actually finds. Grade, toning and pedigree change prices enormously, so check
Heritage, Stack's Bowers and PCGS CoinFacts before buying or pricing.
Format: rank. Coin | denomination; metal; mint or country; diameter mm | Year (negative = BC) | Category
(CENT = US copper and nickel, SILV = US silver, GOLD = US gold, WRLD = world, ANCT = ancient and medieval)
