THE 250 MOST COLLECTIBLE COMICS
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge (CGC census and sold-price lore), not live
price data. Five binder pages of up to 50, by era: Golden Age (1938-1955), Silver Age
(1956-1969), Bronze Age (1970-1984), Modern (1985+), and Indie, Manga & Undergrounds.
First release ships 20 per page (100); each page grows to 50 later without code changes
(append lines, rerun tools/gen_worlds.py comics). Order is roughly most to least desirable.
Newsstand, price variants, signatures and restoration are left to the variant roll in game.
Format: rank. Title #issue | Why it matters | Year | Page | kind (comic / longbox) | tags | Publisher | Key character | Wikipedia titles (; = fallbacks)
Tags: key = first appearance or landmark, one = a #1 issue, poly = sold polybagged.
