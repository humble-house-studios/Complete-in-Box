THE 500 MOST COLLECTIBLE ITEMS IN RETRO GAMING (priority platforms edition)
Compiled Nov 2026 from general collector knowledge, not live price data. Weighted toward NES, SNES, N64, PS1, PS2, GameCube, Game Boy/GBC, GBA, Xbox, Dreamcast and PSP, with a small sampling of Genesis, Saturn, Neo Geo and Atari 2600. Grails first, then by platform; within each platform items run roughly from most to least desirable (approximate, not a strict price ranking). 'Hardware' entries are consoles, controllers and peripherals. Check current sold listings (eBay, PriceCharting, Heritage) before buying or pricing.
